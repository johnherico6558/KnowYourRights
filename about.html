About this project

Why Know Your Rights?

This website is a simple learning space for students who want to understand important human rights in clear, everyday language.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make basic human rights easier to learn, remember, and talk about. Everyone should have access to information that helps them understand their dignity and freedoms.

Why This Website Matters

When people understand their rights, they can recognize unfair treatment, support others, and help build communities based on safety and equality.

Message to Students

Your voice matters. Keep asking questions, treat others with respect, and remember that learning about rights is one way to help make positive change.
