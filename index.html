Understanding Human Rights

What are Human Rights?

Human rights are the basic rights and freedoms every person should have, simply because they are human.

Why should young people know their rights?

Knowing your rights helps you speak up, make informed choices, and ask for help when something is unfair or unsafe.

Why respect everyone's rights?

Respecting other people’s rights helps create communities where everyone can feel included, valued, and safe.

Basic Human Rights

📚

Right to Education

Everyone has the right to learn and go to school.

💬

Freedom of Speech

Everyone has the right to express ideas respectfully.

🧒

Children's Rights

Every child deserves protection, education, and care.

⚖️

Right to Equality

Everyone should be treated fairly regardless of gender, race, or religion.

🛡️

Right to Safety

Everyone deserves to live free from violence and abuse.

❤️

Right to Health

Everyone deserves access to healthcare and a healthy life.
